# Manual detail research

Source: supplied Star Fleet Technical Manual PDF. Page numbers below are 1-based PDF pages. All six PNGs were rendered with Poppler at 2200 px maximum dimension and visually inspected. Geometry depth not dimensioned by the orthographic drawings remains reconstructed, not a measured CAD reproduction.

## Main bridge — pp 94–95, TO:01:06:10 / TO:01:06:11

- Circular outer shell enclosing an annular service corridor and inner bridge. Panel divisions and isolation doors around the ring are explicitly drawn.
- Main visual-display / command axis is **36 degrees offset from ship centerline**. Aft turbolift remains on ship centerline. Do not rotate the entire exterior bridge shell/turbolift by 36 degrees.
- Central command module is a lower circular well enclosed by segmented handrail, interrupted at access steps. Helm/navigation are side by side in front of the captain's chair on the command axis.
- Perimeter is a faceted arc of operator stations with inclined control desks, vertical rectangular display panels and individual pedestal chairs. Named stations: defense/weapons, navigation, command intelligence, communications, engineering and environmental. Engineering/defense subsystem monitors supplement stations.
- Large primary visual display is in the forward-left sector as seen on the plan, aligned to the offset command axis. It is recessed into the inner station wall, not floating above the center.
- A circular aft turbolift projects from the outer ring; it joins an interrupted station arc. An additional exit is at port-forward. Gangway descends ahead on ship centerline; toilet/suit locker are explicitly shown in the ring.
- Cross section confirms shallow curved dome, shallow depressed command floor, raised peripheral stations, lighting cove, above-console piping/wiring trunk, angled console surfaces and dark lower plinths.
- Explicit dimension: **1.90 m headroom limit** in outer service ring. Other dimensions shown by scale only (0–4 m bar). Approximate drawing-derived overall circular shell diameter ~16 m; assign reconstructed depth/clearances accordingly and label approximate.
- Plan color key: platinum SF29UC, red SF10UC, taupe SF28UC, black SF32UC. Cross-section key also identifies orange SF11UC and gray SF30UC. Actual display imagery and button functions are not detailed enough to reproduce literally.

## Impulse unit — p143, TO:03:10:20

- Overall **5.2 m long × 4.5 m wide × 2.8 m high**, model IP186E/2-IR.
- Twin-unit housing widens in top view toward its broad end. Port profile shows differing slope of lower boundary vs upper.
- **12 magnatomic flux circulation tubes**, six along each side; substantial cylindrical tubes bounded by housing rails, not generic flat black rectangles.
- Two elongated field-flux dip constrictions run longitudinally with rounded narrow ends.
- Two firing chambers; rear elevation is drawn on one half of combined front/rear elevation. A square, inward-tapered chamber surround contains concentric circular throat detail.
- Fuel-hopper feed chute on front. **Five** recirculation connections to heat exchangers, shown as small round front ports.
- External vent shields govern reversibility. Internal operating mechanism and exact installation grouping within every starship hull are not fully established by this component sheet. Do not interpret six visible tubes as six exhaust engines.

## Warp nacelles — p144, TO:03:11:20

- Overall **153.619 m length**, **17.3 m maximum diameter**, model SW40/5-3KT.
- Front hemispherical acquisition dome, stepped collar rings, three space-energy-field sensors around collar at **120 degrees**. The drawing's **45 degree** references set particular rotational placements; preserve mirrored inside/outside differentiation.
- Raised block near forward collar: first-stage magnatomic-flux constriction.
- Long asymmetric inboard equipment channel contains the primary reactor/control loop, inlet/outlet flow sensors and successive chiller banks. Counts explicitly **3 power-stage** chillers and **5 main-stage** chillers. Ribs/slots and stepped side profile are drawn.
- Aft external **U-shaped final-stage intercooler** loop running beside the shell; separate rectangular final-stage chiller grille.
- Aft restoration cowl is cut away asymmetrically around a domed field-restoration source. This is visually distinct from the forward hemisphere.
- Sheet differentiates inboard vs outboard views. Appropriate pylon attachments differ for heavy cruiser, dreadnought, scout/destroyer and tug. Unit V rotates 90 degrees to bottom centerline for scout/destroyer. Do not indiscriminately mirror all nacelle details or place every class's pylons identically.
- Precise rib spacing, surface finish, light colors and glow animation are rendering interpretations, not specifications on the sheet.

## Hangar — pp149–150, TO:03:17:00 / TO:03:17:01

- Explicitly **MK-IX model only**; do not claim the same bay for all classes.
- Elongated deck with nearly straight side walls and rounded stern. Deck 19 main bay; Deck 20 maintenance shops below.
- **Six-segment clamshell hangar doors** at curved stern. Curved panels extend from floor perimeter upward under overhead cosmic-ray shield. Exact animated hinge trajectories are not specified.
- Stern fantail lip outside doors; overhead homing beacon/navigation array and cosmic-ray shield are shown in elevation.
- Circular elevator with concentric turntable near middle/forward half. Large maintenance opening below. Side access pockets, forward doors to corridors and elevator lobby, flush gangway hatch and paired aft cargo hatches.
- Observation gallery over/around perimeter, with many rectangular windows. Port elevation confirms elevated gallery, long lighting/duct strip and paired deck-operations towers near stern. Repeat opposite side as P/S labels indicate.
- Paired landing floodlights beneath gallery near stern. Landing tractor-beam room further forward.
- Stern deck spot-landing target is a rectangular four-field marking, not a large generic circular helipad. Circle belongs to elevator/turntable.
- Drawing colors: taupe SF28UC, yellow SF12UC, red SF09UC. Scale is 0–10 m; approximate drawing-derived deck ~20–22 m wide and ~37 m long. No explicit overall dimension callouts on these pages; treat these as diagram-derived proportions.
- Interior cross-sectional thickness and hidden structure are reconstruction choices. A complete shuttle would require additional source pages, not supported by these two hangar sheets alone.
